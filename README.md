# Lao Kemper — LMT & Builder

**Boulder, CO · Health Tech · Clinical Massage Therapy**

---

I'm a licensed and certified massage therapist who got tired of the gap between what clinical bodywork can do and how few people have access to it. So I started building.

My practice is built on a simple premise: most chronic pain and movement limitations have a soft-tissue pattern that can be identified and addressed. I'm now building software to extend that clinical thinking beyond the treatment room.

---

## What I'm Building

**[Boulder Pain Relief Massage](https://boulderpainrelief.com)** — a client-facing web app for my clinical practice.

The centerpiece is **PainMap**: an AI-powered soft tissue assessment tool. You mark where it hurts, select the activities and contexts that trigger it, and receive a plain-language explanation of what's likely happening in your muscles and fascia — along with specific self-care steps and a recommendation on whether to book a session.

The goal isn't to replace clinical judgment. It's to extend the reach of good clinical thinking to people who don't yet have a practitioner they trust.

The app also includes an education library, gated video courses, anatomy guides, and an AI-assisted booking flow.
